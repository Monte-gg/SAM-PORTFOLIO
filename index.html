Research

Optimization of a Localized Plant-Based Milk Blend

Lactose-free milk alternative from local Kenyan cassava, pumpkin seed, white sapote and amaranth leaf.

View details →

Formulated and evaluated a lactose-free plant-based milk alternative using local Kenyan cassava, pumpkin seed, white sapote, and amaranth leaf.

  • Applied fungal alpha-amylase enzymatic liquefaction, reducing apparent viscosity from 18.5 ± 0.6 cP to 4.5 ± 0.3 cP.
  • Fortified with 2.5% w/v Amaranthus cruentus leaf powder, raising protein to 3.4 g/100 mL, calcium to 125.0 mg/100 mL, and lysine score to 56.0 mg/g protein.
  • Executed two-stage high-pressure homogenization (200 bar) and HTST pasteurization (72 °C for 15 s).
  • Conducted proximate/mineral analysis (Kjeldahl, Soxhlet, AAS) with one-way ANOVA and Tukey's post hoc test (p < 0.05).
